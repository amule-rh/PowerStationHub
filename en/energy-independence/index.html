Can I use my electricity bill?

Yes. If you know your monthly kWh, that is the preferred input. If you only know the bill amount, the calculator can convert it using an approximate effective kWh price, but the result will be less precise.

Importante: “Total independence” does not necessarily mean disconnecting from the grid year-round. Real sizing depends on solar irradiation, panel orientation and tilt, shading, seasonality, loads, battery, inverter, regulations and installation conditions. This tool is educational and indicative.
