What makes this Finder different?

Instead of sizing only a battery, it combines consumption, solar generation, storage, power, EV use and backup autonomy. The goal is to give you a starting point for comparing complete home-energy systems.

Important: this is not an engineering design and does not guarantee full grid independence. Real installation requirements depend on solar irradiation, orientation, shading, seasonality, loads, contracted power, regulations, protection devices, compatibility and installer conditions.
