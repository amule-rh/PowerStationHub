Runtime: how to interpret it

Runtime depends on actual consumption and inverter losses. As an editorial rule of thumb, think in terms of Wh ÷ W, but do not treat it as a guaranteed figure. A device drawing 100 W continuously needs about 100 Wh per hour before losses; variable loads such as refrigerators have different cycles and peaks.

Manufacturer-stated charging: Carga completa en tan solo 1 h con Emergency Charge Mode; soporte técnico indica aprox. 1,7 h por CA estándar. Laboratory or ideal-use conditions matter.
