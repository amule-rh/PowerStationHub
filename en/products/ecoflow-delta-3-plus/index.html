What stands out in the specification

  • 1024.0 Wh stated nominal capacity.
  • 1800.0 W stated continuous output.
  • Up to 1000.0 W solar input.
  • LFP / LiFePO₄ battery.

What you should not assume

Nominal Wh does not equal usable AC Wh: conversion losses matter, and efficiency changes with load, temperature and battery state. For motors, compressors and heating loads, always check real and startup power.
