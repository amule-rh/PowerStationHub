How PowerStationHub selects products and calculates its Score

Editorial methodology for comparing portable power stations without turning a score into a purchase promise.

Capacity

Wh used to estimate runtime and battery size.

Output

Continuous W and, when verified, peak output.

Portability

Weight and the balance between capacity and transport.

Solar

Maximum solar input stated by the manufacturer.

What we don't do

  • We don't invent reviews or user ratings.
  • We don't score popularity.
  • We don't treat price as permanent.
  • We don't present runtime estimates as guarantees.

Review date

Product pages show the editorial review date and, where available, the observed price date.
