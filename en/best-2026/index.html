Methodology

How the PowerStationHub Score works

The score is not a laboratory test. It is a comparison tool that weights useful capacity and output, solar input, portability and verified data completeness.

30%

Capacity

More Wh = more potential runtime.

25%

Output

Continuous output for demanding loads.

20%

Solar

Declared maximum solar input.

15%

Portability

Weights portability within the analyzed catalog.

The remaining 10 points cover verified LFP chemistry and technical-data completeness. We will update the formula if we add our own measurements.
