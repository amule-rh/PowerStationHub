W and Wh: what should you calculate?

W (vatios) show the power the station must deliver at a given moment. Wh (vatios-hora) show how much energy the battery can store and how long it can power a load.

Appliance values are indicative. For motors, compressors and high-power heating loads, always check the appliance label and the manufacturer's starting power.

Important: this tool provides an educational estimate, not an electrical measurement. Inverter losses, temperature, battery condition, duty cycles and efficiency can change the result.
