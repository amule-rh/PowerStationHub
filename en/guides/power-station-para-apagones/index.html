Power Station for Power Outages

How to build portable backup for a router, lighting, communications and essential equipment.

Quick rule: Wh mainly determines stored energy; continuous W determines what you can run; peak power matters for startup loads.

How to size it

List the equipment you actually plan to run, estimate watts and hours, and calculate approximate Wh/day. Then check continuous output separately from capacity.

Allow for real-world losses

A simple Wh ÷ W calculation is only a starting point. Conversion losses, the station's own electronics and changing loads reduce usable runtime.

Check solar separately

Solar input is a charging limit, not a guaranteed daily production figure. Actual energy depends on sunlight, orientation, temperature, shading, cabling and the station's input limits.
