Home battery vs portable power station

These products overlap, but they solve different problems: one is integrated into a home's electrical system; the other prioritises portability and flexible backup.

What to check

  • Start with real monthly consumption and the hours when electricity is used.
  • Separate usable capacity from headline capacity.
  • Check continuous and peak inverter power.
  • Model winter and summer separately.
  • Compare total installed cost, not just the battery price.
  • Verify warranty, cycle limits and software support before purchase.
