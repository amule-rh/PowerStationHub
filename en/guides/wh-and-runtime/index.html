Wh, watts and runtime: a simple buying guide

Wh, watts and runtime: a simple buying guide. Guía práctica de capacidad, potencia, autonomía y carga.

1. Estimate your load

List the watts of the devices you want to run and estimate how many hours they will operate. A simple energy estimate is W × hours.

2. Leave headroom

Conversion losses and real-world usage mean you should leave some margin rather than sizing the station exactly to your calculated load.

3. Check solar input

For off-grid use, review solar input power, compatible voltage and expected recharge conditions.

4. Check output

Wh is only part of the picture. Verify continuous output and surge capability for the appliances you plan to connect.

Tip: Specifications and prices change. Always verify the official product page for the exact model before buying.
